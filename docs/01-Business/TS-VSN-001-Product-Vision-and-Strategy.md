# TaxiSphere Product Vision and Strategy

## Executive Summary

TaxiSphere is a cloud-native, multi-tenant SaaS platform for taxi associations. It exists to replace fragmented manual taxi rank operations with a secure digital platform for operations, fleet management, dispatch, finance, reporting, and future passenger services.

## Vision

To become Africa's leading mobility management platform for taxi associations and transport operators.

## Mission

To modernize taxi association operations through reliable, secure, and scalable software that improves operational visibility, financial transparency, compliance, and passenger experience.

## Target Customers

- Taxi associations.
- Rank operators.
- Taxi owners.
- Regional transport operators.
- Future municipal or regulatory stakeholders.

## Strategic Goals

- Support many independent associations from one SaaS platform.
- Provide strict tenant isolation and role-based access control.
- Digitize core rank, driver, vehicle, route, trip, and reporting workflows.
- Build a modular architecture that can evolve without rewriting the product.
- Establish TaxiSphere as a strong enterprise Java and Angular reference project.

## Product Roadmap

| Version | Theme | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | Core Operations | Tenant onboarding, users, associations, drivers, vehicles, routes, trips, and reports. |
| 2.0 | Passenger Services | Passenger portal, route discovery, ticketing, and notifications. |
| 3.0 | Mobile Operations | Driver and passenger mobile experiences. |
| 4.0 | Payments | Digital payments, wallet integration, and settlements. |
| 5.0 | Intelligence | Demand forecasting, route optimization, and operational analytics. |

## Success Measures

- Tenant onboarding can be completed through a controlled workflow.
- Core operational data is isolated per tenant.
- Documents, ADRs, and requirements are traceable.
- The platform can be deployed locally with Docker during early releases.
- The architecture can support future cloud deployment.
